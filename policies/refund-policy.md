# Refund Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to purchases made in the SnippetsHub store, including:
- digital products (snippets, sections, bundles, code files, documentation),
- digital/developer services (e.g., Developer Hours / Expert Install).

## 2. Product nature
Our products are primarily digital and delivered electronically. No physical shipment is provided.

## 3. Consumer withdrawal rights
Consumers may have a statutory 14-day withdrawal right, subject to applicable law.

For digital content not supplied on a tangible medium, the withdrawal right may be lost where:
- performance begins with the consumer’s explicit prior consent, and
- the consumer acknowledges loss of the withdrawal right.

## 4. When a refund may be granted
Because digital products can be copied and deployed immediately, refunds are exceptional and reviewed case-by-case.
A refund may be granted where:
- the product is materially not as described and cannot be reasonably fixed,
- a critical defect prevents intended use,
- duplicate payment was charged for the same order,
- a paid developer service was not delivered as agreed.

## 5. When refunds are generally not granted
Refunds are typically not granted where:
- the product was downloaded, activated, or deployed and the request is based on change of mind,
- the issue comes from third-party apps, custom theme edits, or client-side configuration,
- the selected license tier does not match the buyer’s needs after delivery,
- the request lacks sufficient technical/order details,
- the buyer refuses reasonable troubleshooting cooperation.

## 6. Refund request procedure
Send requests to: {{ contact_email }}.

Please include:
- order number,
- store URL (for Shopify-related products/services),
- detailed issue description,
- supporting material (screenshots, logs, preview links).

Standard response time: up to 3 business days.
SnippetsHub may request additional information before issuing a final decision.

## 7. Effect of an approved refund
If a refund is approved:
- the license is terminated,
- you must stop using the product and delete all copies,
- funds are returned via the original payment method unless law/payment-provider rules require otherwise.

## 8. B2B customers
For B2B transactions, refunds are discretionary unless otherwise required by mandatory law or an explicit written agreement.

## 9. Chargebacks and abuse
Unjustified chargebacks without prior support/refund procedure may be treated as abuse.
Where legally permitted, SnippetsHub may:
- submit complete order and support evidence to the payment processor,
- pursue recovery of outstanding amounts and costs,
- suspend further support/service access.
