# Shopify Policies — SnippetsHub (EN)

This folder contains English policy templates ready to paste into Shopify.  
Replace placeholders in curly braces, e.g. `{{ company_address }}`.

Files:
- `contact-details.md`
- `refund-policy.md`
- `terms-of-service.md`
- `shipping-policy.md`
- `legal-notice.md`
- `privacy-policy.md`

Note:
- Paste each document into the corresponding Shopify policy section.
- Ensure the checkout includes an explicit consent checkbox for immediate delivery of digital content and loss of the right to withdraw.
