# Shipping Policy

Last updated: February 19, 2026

## 1. Delivery Type
SnippetsHub primarily provides digital products and developer services. Delivery is electronic.

## 2. Digital Product Delivery
After payment confirmation, digital products are generally provided via:
- download links,
- order-access files,
- other electronic delivery methods stated in the store.

## 3. Delivery Time
Digital delivery is typically immediate after successful payment.
In rare technical cases, delivery may be delayed. If this happens, contact us at {{ contact_email }}.

## 4. Shipping Costs
No physical shipping applies to digital products, so no physical shipping fee is charged.

## 5. Developer Services
For Developer Hours / Expert Install:
- service is delivered remotely,
- start time and completion depend on access/material readiness and project complexity,
- exact execution details are agreed during service communication.

## 6. No Physical Goods
We do not ship physical products.
