# Refund Policy (Envato-style, adapted to PL/EU law)

## 1. Digital products
We sell digital content and services (snippets, files, documentation). Delivery is electronic and does not include any physical media.

## 2. Right of withdrawal (consumers)
Consumers have a 14‑day right of withdrawal unless:
- they give explicit consent to start delivery of digital content before the 14 days expire, and
- they acknowledge that they lose the right of withdrawal once delivery starts.

If those conditions are met, the right of withdrawal does not apply.

## 3. Refunds by exception (Envato-style)
Due to the nature of digital content, refunds are exceptions and are assessed case‑by‑case. A refund may be granted if:
- the product is **not as described** or does not function as declared and the issue cannot be fixed,
- the product contains a **critical security issue**,
- **support was promised** but not delivered,
- the product **was not downloaded** (if we can verify this).

## 4. Non‑refundable situations (standard Envato-style)
A refund is generally not granted if:
- the customer changed their mind after downloading,
- the product does not meet personal expectations or preferences,
- the purchase was made by mistake,
- the customer lacks the skills to use the product,
- insufficient information is provided to justify the refund,
- the claim is submitted after an unreasonable time since purchase.

## 5. Effect of a refund
If a refund is granted, the license is terminated. The customer must stop using the product and delete all copies.

## 6. How to request a refund
Contact: {{ contact_email }} and include:
- order number,
- store URL,
- detailed issue description,
- supporting materials (screenshots/logs).

## 7. B2B customers
Refunds for business customers are discretionary and do not follow statutory consumer withdrawal rights.
