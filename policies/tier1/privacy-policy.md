# Privacy Policy

## 1. Data controller
The data controller is SnippetsHub - Programming Services. Contact details are provided in “Contact Details”.

## 2. Data we process
Depending on your interaction with the service, we may process:
- identification data (e.g., name, company name),
- contact data (email),
- transaction data (order number),
- technical and analytics data (IP address, browser data, analytics events).

## 2a. Shopify Admin access (no passwords)
SnippetsHub does not request store passwords. Shopify admin access is provided only via Collaborator invite or a dedicated staff account created by the customer.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases
We process data to:
- fulfill orders and deliver digital content,
- handle support requests,
- run analytics (Google Analytics).

Legal bases:
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) — performance of a contract,
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) — legal obligation,
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) — legitimate interest (analytics),
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) — consent (where required).

## 4. Recipients of data
Data may be shared with:
- Shopify (e‑commerce platform),
- payment providers,
- hosting/IT providers,
- Google (Google Analytics).

## 5. Transfers outside the EEA
Some providers may process data outside the EEA (e.g., USA). We use appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses where required.

## 6. Retention period
We retain data as long as necessary to perform the contract, then as required by law (e.g., tax), or until a valid objection is raised.

For data provided to deliver services (store URL, name, email, scope of work), we apply data minimization and delete it after completion unless legal obligations require longer retention.

## 7. Your rights
You have the right to:
- access your data,
- rectify,
- erase,
- restrict processing,
- data portability,
- object,
- withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent).

Complaints can be filed with the Polish Data Protection Authority (UODO) or the relevant authority in your jurisdiction.

## 8. Cookies and analytics
We use cookies for essential site functionality and analytics (Google Analytics). Consent and cookie settings are managed via the cookie banner.
