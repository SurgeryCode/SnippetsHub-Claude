# Privacy Policy

## 1. Data Controller
The data controller is SnippetsHub - Programming Services.
Identification and contact details are provided in "Contact Details".

## 2. Data We Process
Depending on your use of the store, we may process:
- contact/identity data (e.g., name, company, email),
- order and payment-related data (e.g., order number, payment status),
- technical data (e.g., IP address, device/browser information),
- support data (e.g., ticket messages, screenshots, logs),
- project/service data (e.g., Shopify store URL, implementation scope).

## 3. Shopify Access (No Password Requests)
SnippetsHub does not request your store password.
Store access is provided only via:
- Shopify Collaborator access, or
- a dedicated staff account created by you.

We process data to:
- perform and manage contracts (orders, delivery, support),
- comply with legal/accounting obligations,
- handle claims, abuse prevention, and security incidents,
- improve service quality and analytics.

Legal bases (where applicable):
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) contract performance,
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(c) legal obligation,
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(f) legitimate interests (security, analytics, fraud prevention),
- GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) consent.

## 5. Data Recipients
Data may be shared with service providers supporting our operations, such as:
- Shopify,
- payment and billing providers,
- hosting, email, and IT providers,
- analytics and diagnostics providers,
- accounting/legal advisors where necessary.

## 6. International Transfers
Some providers may process data outside the EEA. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses).

## 7. Data Retention
We retain data:
- for the duration of contract performance and support,
- as required by tax/accounting/legal obligations,
- until a valid objection/withdrawal where consent is the legal basis.

Project/service access data is minimized and removed after completion unless legal obligations require longer retention.

We use cookies and similar technologies for:

Cookie settings and consent controls are available in the consent banner/tool.
