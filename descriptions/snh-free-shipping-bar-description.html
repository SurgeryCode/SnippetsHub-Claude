Free Shipping Progress Bar for Shopify That Motivates Customers to Spend More

SnippetsHub Free Shipping Progress Bar is a lightweight, dynamic progress bar for Shopify that shows customers exactly how close they are to earning free shipping — and updates in real time as they add items to their cart. It is perfect for cart pages, cart drawers, mini-carts, and any placement where a spending incentive increases average order value.

With a configurable threshold, custom messages for below and above the goal, and full color control from the Theme Editor, you can match the bar perfectly to your store's branding in minutes. The bar listens to standard Shopify cart update events — including those fired by popular themes like Dawn and Sense — and refreshes automatically without any page reload. Supports a configurable icon, custom placeholder for the remaining amount, and distinct success messaging when the threshold is reached.

This solution is built to support real ecommerce growth by turning free shipping into an active conversion lever, reducing cart abandonment by giving customers a clear, achievable goal, and increasing average order value with zero acquisition cost. If you want a clean, conversion-focused incentive bar that looks professional and works instantly across themes, this is a confident choice for your Shopify store.

Note: Prices in Shopify are stored in cents. The threshold setting uses whole units (e.g., 200 = 200 PLN). Works correctly with all standard currencies.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this automatically update when customers add items?
Yes. The bar listens to standard Shopify cart events and refreshes automatically — no page reload needed. Compatible with Dawn, Sense, Refresh, Craft, and most OS 2.0 themes.

Where should I place it?
Inside a cart drawer for maximum impact, or on the cart page directly above the subtotal. You can also add it to collection pages as a persistent reminder.

Can I change the currency unit?
The threshold is in whole units of your store's base currency. Set 200 for 200 PLN, 50 for $50, etc. No additional configuration needed.

No. Upload files, add the section in Theme Editor, set your threshold and colors. ~10 minutes. Expert Install available at checkout.
