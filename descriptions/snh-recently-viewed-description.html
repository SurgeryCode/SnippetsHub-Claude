Recently Viewed Products for Shopify That Re-engages Browsers and Recovers Lost Sessions

SnippetsHub Recently Viewed Products is a client-side product history section for Shopify that automatically tracks which products a visitor has browsed and displays them as a product grid — on any page, without any server-side logic or app subscription. It is perfect for product pages, the homepage, collection pages, and cart pages where re-surfacing familiar products helps close sessions that would otherwise end without a purchase.

Products are tracked in the visitor's localStorage as they browse your store, and the section renders them instantly using JavaScript. The current product is always excluded on product pages so customers see other items they've explored. The section remains hidden until the visitor has browsed at least one product, so it never shows an empty state to first-time visitors. Number of products shown (2–8) and all colors are configurable from the Theme Editor.

This solution is built to support real ecommerce growth by shortening the path back to products a customer was already interested in, reducing the effort required to compare and reconsider items, and giving returning visitors an immediate visual connection to their browsing history. If you want a lightweight, zero-app recently viewed section that works reliably across sessions, this is a confident choice for your Shopify store.

Note: Products are tracked client-side in localStorage. Data persists until the visitor clears their browser storage. No customer data is sent to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this track customers across devices?
No. The history is stored in the visitor's browser localStorage — it's device and browser specific. No data is synced to any server or customer account.

Does it show products to first-time visitors?
No. The section stays hidden until the visitor has browsed at least one product. It only appears once there is a history to show.

Will it slow down my store?
No. The JS runs after the page is fully loaded and only reads from localStorage — there are no API calls or server requests involved.

Do I need a developer to install this?
No. Upload files, add the section in Theme Editor, configure colors and product count. ~10 minutes. Expert Install available at checkout.
