Upsell & Cross-sell Section for Shopify That Grows Order Value on Every Product Page

SnippetsHub Upsell & Cross-sell Section is a hand-curated "You might also like" product grid for Shopify product pages, designed to surface relevant products and let customers add them to cart in one click — without leaving the page. It is perfect for cross-selling complementary products, upselling higher-margin alternatives, promoting bestsellers alongside niche items, and increasing average order value at the highest-intent moment in the purchase journey.

With a block-based schema in the Theme Editor, you can hand-pick up to 6 products to recommend, add a custom badge per product (e.g., "Best seller", "New"), write a short custom description for each, and set the desktop column count to 2, 3, or 4. Products with compare-at prices automatically display a savings percentage badge. All Add to Cart buttons use AJAX — customers add items instantly without a page reload, seeing the button transition through "Adding…" → "Added!" states for clear feedback.

This solution is built to support real ecommerce growth by presenting the right product at the right moment to a buyer who is already in purchase mode, reducing the need to navigate away from the current product to find complementary items, and increasing cart value with zero extra acquisition cost. If you want a clean, conversion-focused upsell section that gives you full editorial control over what you recommend, this is a confident choice for your Shopify store.

Note: Works on product templates only. Sold-out variants auto-display a disabled "Sold out" button. Up to 6 products per section.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I show different recommendations on different products?
Yes. Create multiple product templates in Shopify and configure each with a different set of recommended products. Assign templates to products in the product editor.

Does it work with Shopify's automatic recommendations?
This section uses hand-picked products only. For algorithm-based recommendations, see SnippetsHub Cart Drawer with Recommendations which uses Shopify's Recommendations API.

What happens when a recommended product sells out?
The Add to Cart button automatically shows "Sold out" and is disabled. The product card remains visible — remove it from the block in Theme Editor when you want to replace it.

Do I need a developer to install this?
No. Upload files, add the section to your product template, click "Add product" for each recommendation in Theme Editor. ~15 minutes. Expert Install available at checkout.
