Product Bundle Builder for Shopify That Increases Average Order Value with Frequently Bought Together

SnippetsHub Product Bundle Builder is a "Frequently bought together" section for Shopify product pages, designed to let customers select and add a curated set of products to their cart in one click — with a live bundle total that shows savings in real time. It is perfect for accessory bundles, starter kits, complementary product sets, gift bundles, and any store looking to increase average order value without a dedicated app.

With a block-based schema in the Theme Editor, you can add up to 5 products to a bundle, set each product as pre-selected or optional, add a short custom note per item (e.g., "Most popular add-on"), and configure a guarantee note below the CTA. The bundle total updates live as customers check and uncheck items, showing both the regular and discounted price when compare-at prices are set in your catalog. One click adds all selected items to the cart via a sequential AJAX request.

This solution is built to support real ecommerce growth by presenting a curated, high-value selection at the moment of purchase intent, making the value proposition of buying together immediately clear, and removing friction from the multi-product add-to-cart flow. If you want a polished, conversion-focused bundle section that looks professional and works reliably, this is a confident choice for your Shopify store.

Note: Up to 5 products per bundle. Works on product and page templates. Highly customized themes may need minor spacing adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can the customer deselect all products?
The first product in the bundle is always selected and cannot be unchecked — it acts as the anchor product. All other products are optional.

How does the savings display work?
Set a "Compare at price" on any bundle product in Shopify Admin. The bundle builder automatically calculates and shows the total regular price vs. bundle price and the savings amount.

Can I show different bundles on different products?
Yes. Add the section to a product template and configure it per template. Use Shopify's template assignment to show different bundles on different product groups.

Do I need a developer to install this?
No. Upload files, add the section to your product template, click "Add bundle product" for each item in Theme Editor. ~15 minutes. Expert Install available at checkout.
