How It Works

  1. Customer completes a purchase and receives their order confirmation email
  2. You add a custom link in the confirmation email pointing to your upsell page (e.g., yourstore.com/pages/special-offer)
  3. Customer lands on the page, sees the offer with countdown timer
  4. Clicking "Claim this offer" takes them directly to Shopify checkout with the upsell product
  5. Clicking "No thanks" returns them to the storefront

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this require Shopify Plus?
No. This works on all Shopify plans. It is a standard Liquid section placed on a custom page — not a native checkout extension.

Will the timer reset if the customer refreshes the page?
No. The timer expiry is stored in sessionStorage and persists throughout the browser session.

How does the "Claim this offer" button work?
It clears the cart, adds the upsell product via AJAX, then redirects directly to /checkout — creating a clean, focused checkout for just the upsell item.

Can I show a discounted price?
Yes. Set a "Compare at price" on the upsell product in Shopify Admin. The card automatically shows the original price crossed out and a "Save X" badge.
