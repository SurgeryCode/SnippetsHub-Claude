Sticky Add to Cart Bar for Shopify That Reduces Scroll-Away Drop-off on Product Pages

SnippetsHub Sticky Add to Cart Bar is a lightweight bar for Shopify product pages that keeps the purchase CTA visible after customers scroll past the main Add to Cart button. It is perfect for long product pages on mobile, stores with detailed descriptions and reviews below the fold, high-ticket items where customers research before buying, and any product page where scroll-away is reducing add-to-cart rate.

With configurable schema settings in the Theme Editor, you can show or hide the product image, title, variant title, and price in the sticky bar, customize the CTA label, set position (top or bottom of screen), control mobile visibility separately, and adjust all colors to match your theme — without writing any code. The bar appears automatically only after the main product form leaves the viewport, so it never competes with the native product layout.

This solution is built to support real ecommerce growth by keeping the Add to Cart action accessible at every scroll depth, reinforcing product context (image, title, variant) while the customer reads reviews or descriptions, and reducing the friction of having to scroll back up to buy. If you want a clean, conversion-focused sticky bar that integrates with your existing product form and works reliably across devices, this is a confident choice for your Shopify store.

Note: Designed for standard Shopify product forms. Bundle builders and heavily customized product forms may require minor integration adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will it show on every product page?
Yes — once added to your product template in the Theme Editor, it appears on all product pages automatically. You can disable it per-section from the Theme Editor.

Does it work with variant selectors?
Yes. The bar reads the currently selected variant from the product form and updates the displayed title, price, and compare-at price accordingly.

Will it conflict with my theme's own sticky header?
The bar is positioned independently. If your theme has a sticky header, set the bar position to "bottom" to avoid overlap.

Do I need a developer to install this?
No. Upload files, add the section to your product template in Theme Editor, configure. ~15 minutes. Expert Install available at checkout.
