Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work with my theme?
Yes. Tested on all major Shopify Online Store 2.0 themes. Highly customized or legacy themes may require minor style adjustments.

What happens when the timer reaches zero?
The timer stops at 00:00:00. You can choose to hide the bar, update the messaging, or replace it with a new campaign — all from the Theme Editor.

Can I use it for evergreen (always-on) urgency?
Yes. Set a rolling end date and update it each time a campaign runs. The timer is fully configurable.

Do I need a developer to install this?
No. Copy the files into your theme, add the section in Theme Editor, set your campaign details. ~10 minutes. Expert Install available at checkout.
