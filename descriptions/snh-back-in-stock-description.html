Back in Stock Notification for Shopify That Captures Demand for Sold-Out Products

SnippetsHub Back in Stock is an email capture section for Shopify product pages that automatically appears when a variant is out of stock and hides when it becomes available again — giving customers a simple way to request a notification without leaving the page. It is perfect for high-demand products with limited inventory, seasonal items that sell out and restock, pre-launch waitlists, and any store looking to recover revenue from sold-out SKUs instead of losing those visitors permanently.

With a configurable endpoint URL in the Theme Editor, you can connect the form to any notification service — Klaviyo's Back in Stock API, a Zapier or Make webhook, a custom App Proxy, or any HTTP endpoint that accepts a JSON POST. The form automatically syncs with variant changes, so when a customer switches between variants, it shows or hides based on real-time availability. Success and error states are handled with clear user feedback, and all colors and labels are fully configurable from the Theme Editor.

This solution is built to support real ecommerce growth by converting out-of-stock visits into future sales instead of permanently lost customers, capturing qualified demand at the exact moment of intent, and giving you an email list of people who already want a specific product. If you want a lightweight, professional back-in-stock capture form that connects to your existing email platform, this is a confident choice for your Shopify store.

Note: This snippet requires an external endpoint (Klaviyo, Zapier, Make, or custom) to actually send email notifications. The snippet handles the capture form — notification delivery is handled by the configured service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this send emails automatically?
The snippet captures the email and sends it to your configured endpoint. The notification service (Klaviyo, Zapier, etc.) handles sending the actual email when the product is back in stock.

Do I need to set up a backend?
You need an endpoint that accepts the form data — but this can be a free Zapier webhook, a Klaviyo integration, or Make. No custom server required for most setups.

Will it automatically hide when the product is restocked?
Yes — on page load and on variant change, the section shows/hides based on the current variant's availability. Restocked products will not show the form to new visitors.

Do I need a developer to install this?
No for the snippet itself. Setting up the notification endpoint (Klaviyo, Zapier) requires following their documentation. Expert Install available at checkout for full setup.
