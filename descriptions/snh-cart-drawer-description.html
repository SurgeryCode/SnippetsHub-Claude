Cart Drawer for Shopify That Keeps Customers in the Shopping Flow

SnippetsHub Cart Drawer is a full-featured AJAX slide-out cart for Shopify, designed to let customers review and edit their cart without ever leaving the product page. It is perfect for stores that want to reduce checkout friction, increase add-to-cart rates, and optionally surface product recommendations directly inside the cart panel.

With a configurable schema in the Theme Editor, you can set the drawer title, enable Shopify-powered product recommendations with a custom heading and item count, toggle payment icons in the footer, and adjust all colors to match your brand — without writing a single line of code. The drawer intercepts standard Shopify add-to-cart form submissions, opens automatically after adding a product, and integrates with common theme cart events fired by Dawn, Sense, Debut, and other OS 2.0 themes.

This solution is built to support real ecommerce growth by keeping customers in the purchase flow after they add items, surfacing relevant upsells without a separate app, and giving instant visual feedback on cart changes. If you want a polished, conversion-focused cart experience that works reliably across devices and themes, this is a confident choice for your Shopify store.

Note: Highly customized themes with proprietary cart systems may require minor integration adjustments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will this conflict with my theme's existing cart?
The drawer is a separate section added to theme.liquid. It coexists with the theme's cart page. You can optionally hide or disable the theme's default cart drawer after installing.

Does it open automatically after "Add to Cart"?
Yes — the drawer intercepts standard Shopify add-to-cart form submissions and opens automatically after the item is added.

Do product recommendations require a Shopify plan?
Recommendations use Shopify's native Product Recommendations API, available on all plans. They're based on purchase history and product relationships configured in your store.

Do I need a developer to install this?
No. Upload files, add one Liquid tag to theme.liquid, configure in Theme Editor. ~15 minutes. Expert Install available at checkout.
